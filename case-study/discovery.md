# Discovery

## How I Identified the Opportunity

The workflow for creating remote job application packages involved repeated activities across multiple candidates.

Rather than treating the work as a series of unrelated document-creation tasks, I examined the process as an operational workflow.

The objective was to identify which parts of the process could be standardized and where AI could assist without removing human judgment.

## Process Observations

The workflow contained several recurring stages:

1. Review candidate information
2. Understand the candidate's professional background
3. Identify relevant strengths and transferable skills
4. Determine appropriate positioning for the target role
5. Create the required application documents
6. Maintain consistency across documents
7. Review the completed package
8. Make corrections when necessary

The repeated nature of these stages suggested that the process could be converted into a reusable system.

## Key Discovery

The biggest opportunity was not simply to use AI to write documents.

The opportunity was to create a defined workflow that could guide the entire process.

This led to the development of a reusable AI-assisted workflow with defined rules for:

- Input
- Analysis
- Positioning
- Document sequencing
- Consistency
- Quality control
- Human review

## Workflow Standardization

One important design decision was to establish a consistent sequence for producing the application package.

Instead of deciding what to create next during every candidate engagement, the workflow establishes a defined progression from the initial candidate information through the final package.

This creates a more predictable operating process.

## AI Opportunity

AI was identified as particularly useful for activities involving:

- Information analysis
- Structuring
- Drafting
- Reformatting
- Consistency checking
- Identifying potential issues
- Supporting repetitive reasoning tasks

However, AI was not intended to replace human responsibility.

The operator remains responsible for reviewing the information, validating the output, making positioning decisions, and approving the final package.

## Design Principle

The discovery process led to a simple principle:

**Do not automate a poorly defined process. First understand and standardize the workflow, then determine where AI and automation can create leverage.**

This principle is central to the broader Founder Associate Systems Lab.
